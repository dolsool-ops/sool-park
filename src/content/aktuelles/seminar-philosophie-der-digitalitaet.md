**Philosophie der Digitalität** · Theorie · Praxis · Kritik · Content

Seminar in the winter term 2026/27, Wednesdays 18–20, Institute of Philosophy, University of Hildesheim. Thirteen sessions, from AlphaGo and cybernetics to large language models, AI ethics and the politics of the platforms.

The course website brings together the guiding question of each session, interviews with people who shape or criticise the AI industry, and the reading list. Participants receive the reader by email.
