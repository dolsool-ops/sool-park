<h2>Stimmen aus dem Go</h2>
<figure class="stimmen-buch">
<img src="/teaching/digitalitaet/bilder/chang_meonjeo-on-mirae.jpg" alt="Umschlag von Chang Kang-myoung, Meonjeo on mirae (Seoul: Dongasia 2025)" width="391" height="600" loading="lazy">

<p class="stimmen-intro">Im März 2016 verlor Lee Sedol in Seoul gegen DeepMinds AlphaGo mit 1:4; sein Sieg in der vierten Partie blieb der einzige eines Menschen gegen AlphaGo in einem offiziellen Wettkampf. Ab 2018 waren starke Go-Programme für alle verfügbar. Chang Kang-myoung, Romancier und früherer Journalist, hat für sein Buch 30 aktive und ehemalige Profis und 6 Go-Fachleute befragt (S. 25). Die Stellen hier sind nicht dem Buch selbst entnommen, sondern den Auszügen und Zitaten, die im Netz stehen (Verlagsauszüge, Rezensionen, Presse); die Seitenzahlen folgen diesen Quellen.</p>
<p class="stimmen-quelle">Stellen aus Chang Kang-myoung, <i>Meonjeo on mirae. AI ihu-ui segye-reul gyeongheomhan saramdeul</i> („Die Zukunft, die früher kam. Menschen, die die Welt nach der KI schon erlebt haben“), Seoul: Dongasia 2025, und Aussagen von Lee Sedol in der Presse. Englisch: Arbeitsübersetzung von Sool Park; die koreanischen Originale stehen in den angegebenen Quellen.</p>

<figure class="stimme">

<blockquote lang="en">After the games with AlphaGo, what I came to question was not so much my understanding of Go as things like human creativity and what the old Go proverbs say. These doubts arose as I watched AlphaGo play moves that differed from everything we had known.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: OSEN, 15.3.2016 (osen.co.kr/article/G1110374279)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Lee Sedol zu seinem Rücktritt, November 2019</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Being the number one in Go meant the pride of being the one who plays Go best in the world. But once AI appeared, I felt that however well I played, I could not win. (…) Now even professionals learn Go from AI. We cannot win a single game. We end up copying [the AI]. If a master far stronger than me appeared, I would gladly learn from that person, but…</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: Seoul Shinmun, 27.11.2019 (m.seoul.co.kr/news/2019/11/27/20191127500015)</p>

<figure class="stimme">

<p class="quelle">Quelle: Verlagstext zu <i>Meonjeo on mirae</i>; brunch.co.kr/@hyun1108/61</p>

<p class="quelle">Quelle: brunch.co.kr/@hyun1108/61 (ohne Seitenangabe)</p>

<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Ha Ho-jeong 4-dan im Gespräch mit Chang (S. 280–281)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Because of AlphaGo the Go industry was destroyed and the authority of professional players declined. I was sad: ‘Why is the Go I loved now judged by numbers?’ But everything has its pros and cons, doesn’t it? The shock AlphaGo delivered is sad in itself, but I think it would be even sadder to have died without knowing the moves AlphaGo showed us. There were so many creative and beautiful moves. We call it ‘Teacher Al’ [al sabeom-nim], you know. I don’t want to go back to that world of ignorance in which we didn’t know those moves.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: brunch.co.kr/@jaydenkang/417</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Ha Ho-jeong 4-dan, Nachricht an Chang am nächsten Tag (S. 281)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">[The day after this conversation, Ha Ho-jeong 4-dan sent me a message reporting a change of mind.] I’m changing my answer: the days without AI were much better. I miss the old days when we played a Go of romance. Back then, when we studied a new move, people’s effort and passion were soaked into it. We cheered the new moves we found that way and grew by studying them again and again. We humans are imperfect, but there was a romance in growing within that imperfection – and since that damn AlphaGo, something has gone cold. As technology advances, many people’s bodies get more comfortable while their souls seem to wither, and today’s Go doesn’t seem much different, so I say it again.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: brunch.co.kr/@jaydenkang/417</p>
</figure>
<h3>3 Anpassung</h3>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang über Shin Jin-seo 9-dan (S. 69)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Shin Jin-seo 9-dan differs greatly from senior players such as Cho Hun-hyun, Lee Chang-ho and Lee Sedol: he took not a human but an AI as his teacher.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: yes24.com/product/goods/148032297 (Verlagsauszüge)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Eine befragte Person über den Unterricht an Go-Schulen (S. 212)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Teachers aren’t needed anymore, are they? You just set up an AI machine and let the students talk among themselves. The kids know the right answers from the AI. The teachers who denied those right answers were the first to be weeded out. The moment the kids stop respecting you, you’re out. First the teachers who told stories about ‘how it was in my day’ were weeded out, then those who tried to teach the right answers. Now we teach by discussion: the teacher teaches for one minute and then spends nine minutes asking ‘Why did you play this here, why that there?’ and letting the kids answer. Teaching has changed from telling the right answer to checking the reasoning that leads to it. The academies for training professionals that couldn’t adapt to that have closed.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: brunch.co.kr/@sejinjung/5</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang über die Logik der Einführung (S. 102–103)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Until AI is introduced, the people working in an industry and those hoping to enter it may agree among themselves to reject it. But once AI is actually introduced, a group emerges that benefits from it. Its members will see the change AI has brought as positive, as desirable.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: yes24.com/product/goods/148032297 (Verlagsauszüge)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Verlagstext zum „menschlichen Go“ (nicht Changs Wortlaut)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Even after AlphaGo, some professionals said they would play ‘human Go, the kind AI cannot play’. But ask them what exactly human Go is, and the answers are elements that have nothing to do with excellence: misjudgements and mistakes, facial expressions and gestures that betray emotion, the stories surrounding a contest.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: Verlagstext, yes24.com/product/goods/148032297</p>
</figure>
<h3>4 Changs These: Die Zukunft kam zuerst ins Go</h3>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang (S. 25–26)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">I think the future came to the Go world first. What happened in the Go world over several years from 2016 on will happen in many industries. An AI suddenly appears that does better the work to which people devoted decades because they believed it had some value. That AI spreads at a low price. People are forced to ‘coexist’ with it. They have to follow the new order the AI creates. The AI redefines concepts you knew, and you have to learn them anew. AI is not like the typewriter or the word processor.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: brunch.co.kr/@jaydenkang/417</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang (S. 26)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Even if we stop the Terminator and keep our jobs, some human values will surely be broken in the process. (…) And we will probably recognize what those values were only belatedly, after the destruction has happened.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: yes24.com/product/goods/148032297 (Verlagstext)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang (S. 47)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">Whatever writing a novel requires – creativity, literary quality, or anything else – there is no ground for saying that only humans can have it. That is precisely the lesson of AlphaGo.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: yes24.com/product/goods/148032297 (Verlagsauszüge)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
<figcaption>Chang (S. 187)</figcaption>
<blockquote lang="en">I see people saying things like: ‘AI is just a tool; everyone can choose whether to use it, and whether they use it or not, they can hold on to the values they pursue.’ Their naive outlook is wrong. Even if I don’t use AI, the values I pursue change and are overturned because of other people who do.</blockquote>
<p class="quelle">Quelle: yes24.com/product/goods/148032297 (Verlagsauszüge)</p>
</figure>
<figure class="stimme">
